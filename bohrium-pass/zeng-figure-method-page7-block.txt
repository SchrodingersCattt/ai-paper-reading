QM calculation. The potential energy and atomic forces for every structure in the final dataset were calculated by Gaussian 16 \(^{72}\) software at the MN15/6-31G** level. The MN15 functional was employed because it has broad accuracy for multi-reference and single-reference systems \(^{73}\). To consider the spin polarization effect, the initial wave function of a given structure is obtained by the combination of the wave functions of individual molecular species forming the structure, while the wave function of each molecular species was calculated based on its own charge and spin.
